# Contributing

By submitting a contribution, you retain your copyright and agree to provide
prose, diagrams, and knowledge metadata under CC BY-SA 4.0 and repository-local
source code under MPL 2.0. No copyright assignment is required.

Imported material must have a provenance record. Record the source project,
source revision, author or requested attribution, permission or compatible
license, and all material modifications. Do not add third-party embeds or media
merely because an imported page links to them.

AI-assisted and imported drafts require human review of facts, sources,
relationships, conversion quality, and licensing before `published` status.
